# 🚀 Selected Work

### 🛰️ ForestSentinel

**Satellite Imagery × Deep Learning**

Using **SegFormer-B2** and Sentinel-2 imagery to classify land cover and identify early-stage wildfire-risk zones across British Columbia and Alberta.

`PyTorch` · `SegFormer` · `Sentinel-2` · `GeoPandas` · `Google Earth Engine`

**→** [Explore the project](https://github.com/SawwmyaP/ForestSentinel)

---

### 🧠 SRM Career Catalyst

**NLP × RAG × Career Intelligence**

An AI resume analysis engine combining **semantic embeddings, clustering and RAG**, built around career archetypes derived from 200+ alumni profiles.

`FastAPI` · `LangGraph` · `Sentence Transformers` · `FAISS` · `K-Means`

**→** [Explore the project](https://github.com/SawwmyaP/SRM-Resume-Expert)

---

### 🅿️ ExpressPark

**Computer Vision × Smart Infrastructure**

An AI-ready smart parking platform for **SRM KTR Campus**, combining real-time slot visualization, dashboards, multilingual support and intelligent occupancy detection.

`Next.js` · `React` · `PostgreSQL` · `Supabase` · `YOLO` · `OCR`

**→** [Explore the project](https://github.com/SawwmyaP/Express-Park)
