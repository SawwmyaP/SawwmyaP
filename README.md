## 🏎️ About Me

> **I like my code the way F1 likes its cars — fast, precise, and built to perform under pressure.**

I'm a **Computer Science undergraduate specializing in AI & ML at SRMIST**, building at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, software engineering and real-world systems**.

My work spans **LLMs, NLP, Computer Vision, Deep Learning, geospatial AI and full-stack development** — from analyzing satellite imagery for wildfire risk to building intelligent resume systems and AI-ready infrastructure.

When I'm not building, you'll probably find me watching an F1 race, overanalyzing strategy, or wondering why the pit wall made *that* decision. 🏎️

* 🤖 **AI/ML Systems** — Deep Learning, NLP, LLM applications & intelligent workflows
* 👁️ **Computer Vision** — Segmentation, object detection & image understanding
* 🛰️ **Geospatial AI** — Satellite imagery, Sentinel-2 & wildfire-risk detection

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🛰️ ForestSentinel

**Satellite Image Segmentation for Wildfire Risk Detection**

Fine-tuning **SegFormer-B2** on Sentinel-2 satellite imagery to classify land cover and identify early-stage wildfire-risk zones across British Columbia and Alberta.

`PyTorch` `SegFormer` `Sentinel-2` `Google Earth Engine` `GeoPandas` `Plotly Dash`

[→ Explore ForestSentinel](https://github.com/SawwmyaP/ForestSentinel)

---

### 🧠 SRM Career Catalyst

**AI-Powered Resume Intelligence Engine**

An NLP system combining **semantic embeddings, clustering and RAG** to analyze resumes against career archetypes derived from 200+ alumni profiles.

`FastAPI` `LangGraph` `Sentence Transformers` `FAISS` `K-Means`

[→ Explore SRM Career Catalyst](https://github.com/SawwmyaP/SRM-Resume-Expert)

---

### 🅿️ ExpressPark

**AI-Ready Smart Parking Management System**

A full-stack intelligent parking platform built for **SRM KTR**, combining real-time parking visualization, RBAC dashboards, multilingual support, YOLO-based occupancy detection and OCR.

`Next.js` `React` `PostgreSQL` `Supabase` `YOLO` `OCR`

[→ Explore ExpressPark](https://github.com/SawwmyaP/Express-Park)
